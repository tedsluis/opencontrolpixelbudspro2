## Executive summary

**Verdict.** The project is in good technical health where it matters most: the build, 1,652 unit tests and lint are green; the app sends only
what the ADRs unblock (8 `transport.send` sites, DLCI 0x04/0x02 only, every write through the Safe-Mode gate); no network, GMS or telemetry; every
🟢 FACT frame cited in `PROTOCOL.md` was re-run and matches the bytes; every numeric claim of sessions 0051…0057 checked here holds. **No critical finding.**

The debt is in the *record*, not the code: 54 findings — **0 critical, 5 major, 40 minor, 9 nit** — plus 5 new protocol leads.

- **Five majors.** (1) The binding texts (`AGENTS.md` §8/§11, ADR-001) still require ViewModels and a `BudsUiState` that were deliberately never built,
  without a superseding ADR (GOV-01). (2) `AGENTS.md` §5's first battery mechanism, `ACTION_BATTERY_LEVEL_CHANGED`, is not public API (GOV-02). (3) ADR-024 keeps a
  🟢 "Settable = dock state" decision that its own Updates refute (PROT-01). (4) A 🟢 FACT in `CAP-002-FINDINGS.md` calls the Buds "a different device" (CAP-01).
  (5) Several "clean negative" capture conclusions rest on a tshark filter that errored or matched nothing — the CAP-029 AVRCP pause, CAP-044's "zero DISC",
  CAP-047's "Option E never fires" (CAP-02).
- **Systemic minors.** Capture FINDINGS files are not revisited after promotions (A58-CAP-04), rule 9a addenda keep accumulating (CAP-05, GOV-06),
  deferred documentation from one session is never picked up by the next (SES-04: `TESTPLAN` has no `CAP-063` pointer, `CAP-064` is not in the capture plan),
  and "read in full" of 1.36 MB is routinely — and honestly — not done (SES-03).
- **App defects (minor).** The ANC tile can stay on "Open the app" while connected (APP-01); an unreadable settings answer is reported as a timeout
  (APP-03); a non-atomic state transition and a method-only reply match are small races (APP-05, APP-04); the EQ sliders can write four assumed bands (APP-02,
  a decision).
- **New leads.** The pw_hdlc address is computable from (core, component) and the channel names the hosting bud (L-1); the "unnamed" services are Dosimeter,
  Multipoint, BundledUpdate, UpdateHelperService and DynamicServerConfig — Dosimeter streams a live hearing-exposure value (L-4); "1779298694" is a firmware
  version, not a serial (L-5).

**Top five actions** (maintainer decisions marked in the action list): decide ViewModel vs as-built and record it; supersede ADR-024's FACT; correct the false
FACT and the false negatives in the capture files; fix `AGENTS.md` §5's battery list; one FEATURE session for APP-01/03/04/05.
