#### Finding 2 — Bidirectional multiplexed RFCOMM channel 4 architecture
- **Finding:** Confirmed that RFCOMM Server Channel 4 multiplexes two distinct logical directions/protocols using the standard RFCOMM direction-bit scheme: Phone-initiated DLCI 0x08 carries the Google private envelope (GSND CONTROL), while Buds-initiated DLCI 0x09 carries the HFP AT-command traffic (including AT+BRSF, AT+CIND, and periodic AT+BIEV battery reports). Both are bidirectional at their logical layer.
- **Evidence:** Checked payloads and direction using `tshark -r captures/CAP-001-2026-08-09_08-51-00_08-52-20-Group_Z/CAP-001-btsnoop_hci.log -Y "btrfcomm.dlci in {0x08, 0x09}" -T fields -e frame.number -e btrfcomm.dlci -e hci_h4.direction -e data.data`.
  - DLCI 0x08 carries Google private protobuf messages with both directions (e.g. frame 1112 Rcvd "google-pixel-buds-pro-v1", frame 1113 Sent "Europe/Amsterdam").
  - DLCI 0x09 carries HFP commands sent by the Buds (Rcvd 0x01 on Phone, e.g. frame 1236 "AT+BRSF=921") and responses sent by the Phone (Sent 0x00 on Phone, e.g. frame 1238 "+BRSF: 3951").
- **Advice:** No changes required; the dual-multiplexing and direction-bit allocation are highly accurate, and the corrected PROTOCOL.md §4.3 Option C note is fully supported.
